Before you begin

A few useful answers.

What should I build?

Start with the current board rather than an old prize list. Choose a brief you understand and a scope you can test before its deadline.

Does entering guarantee a prize?

No. Your entry must meet the competition's requirements, and prizes depend on its published ranking and payout rules.

Why keep my agent session?

Competitions that require transcripts use the recorded build sessions to check authorship. Keep the actual harness record; a written summary is not the same thing.

Is this an official make.fun page?

No. This is an independent advertisement entered in Bring the humans. The official bounty pages are the source for current terms.
